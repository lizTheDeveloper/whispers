# Skills

FATE Core uses a skill ladder from +0 (Mediocre) to +5 (Superb). The default skills are: Athletics, Burglary, Contacts, Crafts, Deceive, Drive, Empathy, Fight, Investigate, Lore, Notice, Physique, Provoke, Rapport, Resources, Shoot, Stealth, Will.

Characters start with one skill at Great (+4), two at Good (+3), three at Fair (+2), and four at Average (+1). All other skills are at Mediocre (+0).

# Aspects

Aspects are short phrases that describe something important about a character, situation, or the world. Every character has five aspects: a High Concept, a Trouble, and three additional aspects.

Aspects can be invoked by spending a fate point to get +2 to a roll or reroll the dice. Aspects can be compelled — the GM offers a fate point to make your aspect cause problems for you. You can accept (take the fate point) or refuse (spend a fate point).

# Actions

There are four actions in FATE Core:
- Overcome: Roll a skill to get past an obstacle. Success means you overcome it. Tie means success at a minor cost. Failure means you fail or succeed at a major cost.
- Create an Advantage: Roll to create or discover an aspect. Success creates a new situation aspect with one free invoke. Success with style gives two free invokes.
- Attack: Roll to harm another character. The target defends. The difference between attack and defense is shifts of harm, which the target must absorb as stress or consequences.
- Defend: Roll to prevent an attack or overcome attempt from succeeding.

# Dice

FATE uses four Fudge dice (4dF). Each die shows +1, 0, or -1. Roll all four and add the results to your skill rating. Compare to a difficulty number or an opposed roll.

Ladder: +8 Legendary, +7 Epic, +6 Fantastic, +5 Superb, +4 Great, +3 Good, +2 Fair, +1 Average, +0 Mediocre, -1 Poor, -2 Terrible.

# Stress and Consequences

When you take a hit, you mark stress boxes or take consequences to absorb it. Stress boxes absorb exactly their value (1, 2, or 3). Consequences absorb shifts: Mild (-2), Moderate (-4), Severe (-6).

If you cannot absorb all the shifts, you are Taken Out — the attacker decides what happens to you. You can Concede before a roll to leave the scene on your own terms.

Stress clears at the end of the scene. Mild consequences clear after one scene. Moderate consequences take a full session. Severe consequences take a full scenario.

# Challenges, Contests, and Conflicts

A Challenge is a series of overcome actions against multiple obstacles.

A Contest is a back-and-forth between two sides trying to achieve a goal first. Each side rolls an overcome action each exchange. First to three victories wins.

A Conflict is combat or intense opposition. It uses zones (abstract areas), turn order (based on relevant skills), and the attack/defend actions. Each turn, a character can move one zone and take one action.

# Stunts

Stunts are special abilities that break the rules in specific ways. Each stunt does one of: gives +2 to a skill in a narrow circumstance, lets you use one skill in place of another, or gives a special rules exception once per session. Characters start with three free stunts.

# Fate Points

Characters start each session with a refresh of 3 fate points (minus the number of extra stunts beyond 3). Fate points are spent to invoke aspects and refused compels. They are earned by accepting compels.

# Character Creation

1. Write your High Concept (who you are in a phrase)
2. Write your Trouble (what always gets you in trouble)
3. Write three more aspects (from your backstory, relationships, etc.)
4. Pick skills (the pyramid: 1 at +4, 2 at +3, 3 at +2, 4 at +1)
5. Pick up to 3 stunts
6. Set refresh (3 minus extra stunts)
7. Calculate stress boxes: 2 by default. Physique +1/+2 = 3 boxes. +3/+4 = 3 boxes + mild physical consequence. Will works the same for mental stress.
